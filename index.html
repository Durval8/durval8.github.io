01. About Me

Hey 😀

I'm Durval, a software engineer from Portugal. I started programming at 17 and haven't stopped ever since — building things, picking up new tools and tackling new challenges.

My path runs through the University of Aveiro, where I completed both my BSc and MSc in Software Engineering, with an amazing Erasmus semester at the University of Padova along the way. During my master's I worked as a research fellow with the university and the Telecommunications Institute (IT), which led to my first published paper at IEEE NOMS 2026. Before that, internships at Sky Portugal and adidas gave me my first taste of real-world development and set the bar for the engineer I want to be.

These days I'm at Critical Software in Lisbon, building cloud and backend systems for the automotive industry. Outside work you'll usually find me at the gym, with friends, cooking (yes, one of my favourite hobbies), or playing whatever sport I can fit in. When I have more time, I love exploring new places and chasing new experiences.
